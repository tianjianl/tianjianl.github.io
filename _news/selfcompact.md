Our new work: [Self-Compacting Language Model Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23525) is out! In this work, we let the model itself decide when and how to compact its own context — pairing a compaction tool with a lightweight rubric for when to fire and when to hold off — matching or beating fixed-interval summarization at 30-70% lower cost.
